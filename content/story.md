# JMHS Marching Band Attack

*Story bible (working title). Fighter files are in `content/characters/<id>.json`.*

## Premise

Every fall, once the last home game is over and the stadium lights are still humming, the JMHS marching band holds a secret tradition: **Band Wars**, a no-holds-barred tournament (school-appropriate, of course) to settle which section really runs the band.

The prize is the **Golden Plume**, a gold shako plume passed down from champion to champion. The winner picks the halftime feature for next season and the post-game playlist, and gets free food at the fast food place for the rest of the year. Most important, they get bragging rights forever.

Nine band members enter. The tournament moves through three spots every JMHS band kid knows:

1. **The Locker Room**: pre-show nerves, instrument cases, lockers slamming. This is where challenges are made and the first rounds are fought.
2. **The Fast Food Restaurant**: the post-game hangout, with booths, fries and a menu board. Rivalries boil over between the counter and the drink machine.
3. **The Football Field at Night**: stadium lights, empty bleachers and yard lines. The finals are fought on the 50-yard line.

## The Final Boss

When the champion is crowned at midnight, the stadium lights flicker and a whistle echoes across the field. **Major Discord**, a legendary former JMHS drum major who lost the very first Band Wars, marches out of the dark end zone to claim the Golden Plume for himself. Only the beat of the whole band can bring him down.

## Arcade Mode Flow (suggested)

1. Intro text: *"The last home game is over. The lights are still on. Band Wars begins."*
2. Rounds 1–3 in the **Locker Room**.
3. Rounds 4–6 at the **Fast Food Restaurant**.
4. Rounds 7–8 on the **Football Field at Night**.
5. Boss: **Major Discord**, on the football field at midnight.
6. The fighter's `ending` from their JSON file.

## Tone

Fun, competitive and school-appropriate. Nobody really gets hurt, and every "KO" is a fighter who's out of breath, out of tune or out of step. Fights are about pride in your section, and in the end the band is one team.
